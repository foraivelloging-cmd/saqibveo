How Device-linked Tracking Works

This client helps developers integrate, test, and preview physical-level shortlink tracking capabilities. When you create a short URL, it binds locally to your browser's persistent deviceId. Anyone visiting the link you generate will have their detailed ambient telemetry (GPS, battery, screen specs, WebGL renderer, and camera imagery optionally) sent back to this server. You can refresh and track clicks at any time below.
